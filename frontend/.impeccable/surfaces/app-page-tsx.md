# Surface brief: app shell and all dashboard pages

Mode: Operate. Visitor: Builderflow founders/product leads reading an outside growth assessment, presented live then explored.
Task: see the evidence first, then the AI recommendation that rests on it; click any claim to its source.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A construction drawing set. The dashboard is a numbered sheet index; every claim is a callout that points to its source. Refuses the generic white-card SaaS dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue sidebar (#0E2A47) with sheet numbers (A-01..A-06) in mono. Paper-cool ground (#F5F8FC), hairline blueprint-blue rules (#B9CBE0), drawing-blue accent (#1D5FB8). Redline red (#D2362B) marks gaps and not-found. Highlighter amber marks partial. Green marks have-it. State also carries a glyph. Hanken Grotesk UI, Geist Mono for sheet numbers, codes and callouts. Page headers are title blocks. Citations are numbered callout bubbles.

STORY: Reader sees the numbers and gaps, understands where Builderflow is exposed, opens a recommendation and sees the exact rows behind it, clicks through to the real source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: 232px ink sidebar, product mark, six sheet-numbered nav items, generated-at stamp. Right: title block (sheet A-01, Overview, revision date, source count), KPI row of four, below it gap-by-tier and pain-by-theme as bar strips on one shared scale, then top quick wins linking back to those signals. Primary action: open a recommendation.
