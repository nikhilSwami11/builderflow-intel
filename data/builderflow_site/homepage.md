# Builderflow public website (homepage)

## Positioning
- Tagline: "Stop Losing Leads Start Booking More Jobs"
- All-in-one construction management platform for remodelers, contractors and home improvement professionals: leads, follow-ups, projects, invoicing, payments.
- Company: "A Venture of SNS System Inc.", McKinney, TX.

## Problems it claims to solve
- 60-80% of leads lost to poor follow-up
- Teams run on spreadsheets, sticky notes and text messages
- Missed appointments and estimate follow-ups
- No clear view of the job pipeline

## Target customers
- Remodelers, contractors, home improvement pros
- FAQ: all trades, including remodeling, roofing, restoration, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, flooring, painting, windows/doors, solar, handyman

## Modules listed in navigation (live vs coming soon not stated on the site)
- Lead Intake Automation
- Builder Estimate Pro
- Builder Claim Scope
- Builder FSM (field service management)
- Builder Finance
- Work Orders Management
- Technician Mobile App
Note: the portal (portal.builderflow.app) says only Claim Scope is live today, with CRM, estimates and finance "rolling out next".

## Feature groups on the homepage
- Lead Management & CRM: lead capture, automated follow-up sequences, SMS/email automation, lead scoring and qualification
- Project Management: estimate-to-completion tracking, crew and subcontractor scheduling, real-time updates, document management
- Financial Management: estimates and proposals, invoices, payment tracking, QuickBooks integration
- Other: branded client portal, professional proposals, single dashboard for multiple crews and clients, mobile-first

## Claimed outcomes (vendor claims)
- 60-80% more leads converted, 40% more jobs booked, 15+ hours saved weekly, faster payments

## Pricing
- Plans: Starter, Professional, Premium
- "Starting at" shown with no dollar figure on the homepage
- Includes: full CRM + project management, unlimited users, all integrations, white-glove onboarding, "no hidden fees"
- Go-live takes 2-4 weeks, training included
