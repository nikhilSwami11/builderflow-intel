The Problem: Paper-Based Chaos

Restoration and remediation companies face critical operational challenges

Paper-Based Inefficiencies
Field technicians spend 3-4 hours daily on paperwork instead of billable work
Handwritten reports are illegible and incomplete
Physical documents lost, damaged, or delayed in transit
Manual transcription creates errors and 24-48 hour delays
Data Fragmentation
Job information scattered across email, spreadsheets, and multiple platforms
Zero real-time visibility into field progress or technician locations
Client updates delayed until paperwork returns to office
Status tracking requires constant phone calls
Communication Delays
Clients wait 48-72 hours for inspection results
Urgent issues not identified until paperwork is processed
Missing documentation affects insurance claim processing
